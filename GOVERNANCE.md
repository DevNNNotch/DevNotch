# Governance

DevNotch is maintained in public. Maintainers are responsible for technical direction, releases, security response, and community moderation.

## Decisions

- Small, reversible changes are decided through pull request review.
- New data providers, persistent data collection, network listeners, and permission changes require a public design discussion before implementation.
- Security fixes may be developed privately until disclosure is safe.
- Provider support requires a documented and testable data source. Estimates and undocumented scraping are not accepted as authoritative usage data.

## Maintainers

The initial repository owner is the final decision maker until at least two active maintainers exist. New maintainers are selected based on sustained, constructive contributions and security judgment. No external member is granted repository access without explicit owner approval.

## Conduct

Participation is governed by `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
