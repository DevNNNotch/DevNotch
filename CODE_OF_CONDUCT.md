# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Constructive disagreement about product and engineering decisions is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, and publication of private credentials or source code are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- Giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback.
- Respecting privacy and security boundaries.
- Acknowledging tradeoffs and correcting mistakes openly.
- Helping contributors reproduce and verify results.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the Organization owners. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior.

Maintainers must handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid conflicts of interest. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
